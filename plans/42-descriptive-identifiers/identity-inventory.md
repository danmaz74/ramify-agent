# Plan 42 identity delivery inventory

This inventory tracks semantic planning identities and the contracts that carry them. A run captures its identity contract in `job.json`; absent means legacy. The installed fresh-run default remains `legacy` until all producers and consumers are ready. Historical records keep their original bytes and IDs.
