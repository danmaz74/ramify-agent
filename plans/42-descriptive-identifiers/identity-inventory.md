# Plan 42 identity delivery inventory

This inventory tracks semantic planning identities and the contracts that carry them. A run captures its identity contract in `job.json`; absent means legacy. The installed fresh-run default remains `legacy` until all producers and consumers are ready. Historical records keep their original bytes and IDs.

Invocation, session, operation, event, audit receipt, attempt and iteration IDs are technical identities. External tests and checks, module paths, exported symbols and user-supplied plan/project names keep their established contracts. Iteration 4 verifies public lookup and display for all accepted families. Iteration 5 enables the complete descriptive contract for fresh runs and verifies continuation with both captured contracts.
