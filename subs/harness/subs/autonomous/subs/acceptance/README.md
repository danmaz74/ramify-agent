# Acceptance

Owns candidate qualification and committing gates; review preparation, admission
and settlement; finding reconciliation; obligation satisfaction; nonfunctional
assessment and repair qualification; and completion eligibility. It evaluates one
retained candidate/evidence basis rather than treating a passing check as a
completed consumer integration.

`Acceptance` receives captured identity/configuration and detached, versioned
observations. It owns review queues, orientation promises and preparation abort
controllers. Lifecycle supplies task execution, current authority, accepted
publication and named effect ports; no Run, RunLog, mutex or live map crosses the
boundary. Publication callbacks decide against the current observation inside
lifecycle's accepted-transition serialization. Finding effects retain an opaque
prepared carrier from the existing finding transition, preserving the atomic
assessment plus durable brief effect. Correction close still commits the iteration
and its finding transitions together.

`CommittingGates` and `AfterHandling` bind those same immutable reads and explicit
publication/effect operations for managed and manual workflows. A manual workflow
supplies its own journal adapter and explicit refusals for operations it cannot
authorize; it does not construct or inspect autonomous private state.

The exact handback gate record, candidate tree, review/finding completion basis
and reconciliation identity survive interruption and are rechecked before the
serialized transition. Historical verification events without the later optional
basis retain their compatibility path. Review work has no elapsed lifetime or
silence deadline: stop, shutdown, genuine failure and settlement remain explicit.
The autonomous final gate still requests full nested verification; the refactor's
configured audit retains its separate default selection.
